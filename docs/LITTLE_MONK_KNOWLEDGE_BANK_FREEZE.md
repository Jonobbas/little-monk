# Little Monk Knowledge Bank — Source-Scope Freeze v1.0

**Decision date:** 10 October 2026  
**Status:** Frozen source universe; intake closed  
**Applies to:** Japanese Principles, Behaviour Science, cross-cultural research, qualitative/lived-experience research, books, articles, and related source records currently listed in Little Monk's knowledge bank.

## Freeze decision

The knowledge bank is now a **closed v1.0 collection**. Little Monk will not ingest additional books, research papers, articles, websites, datasets, public posts, or other material from public information sources during this phase. The existing curated records are the complete source universe for v1.0.

This freezes the **source scope**. It does not mean that every listed item is verified, fully read, synthesized, or approved for use by the agent. Existing admission statuses remain unchanged. Conditional, provisional, queued, or duplicate records must continue to be labelled accordingly and must not be treated as approved agent knowledge.

## What work may continue inside the freeze

- Organize and classify the already-listed materials.
- Resolve duplicates among existing records without silently deleting their provenance.
- Review and synthesize only materials already in the frozen source universe.
- Improve source notes, evidence labels, limitations, and design interpretations for those materials.
- Correct the metadata of an existing record when needed to identify that same source; record the correction without expanding the collection or adding new substantive claims.
- Develop Little Monk's own design hypotheses from the frozen materials and label them as hypotheses.

## What is paused

- Searching public information for additional sources, examples, claims, data, or trends to add to the bank.
- Adding newly discovered books, papers, articles, public webpages, or datasets, even if they appear relevant.
- Expanding existing notes with claims from sources outside the frozen collection.
- Allowing the agent to browse the open web or retrieve unreviewed candidate material as knowledge-bank content.

## Current baseline

The frozen corpus is the material already present in the project catalogue and research register, including:

- the original 28-book Japanese-principles core and the listed wider-perspective books;
- the research and evidence records already present in the Japanese-principles review and source register;
- the Behaviour Science candidate set, including the six inherited provisional entries and their identified overlaps;
- existing cross-cultural and lived-experience notes already recorded in project documents.

The existing catalogues and register are the authoritative inventory. This policy does not add or approve any source.

## Reopening the intake

The source freeze stays in force until the project owner explicitly reopens intake. A future version should state its scope, reason for reopening, allowed source categories, review requirements, and version/date. No automatic web discovery or background ingestion is permitted.

## Product boundary

Little Monk's knowledge bank is a curated source set, not a live internet search service. The agent may use only approved records from this corpus, with source type and evidence limits preserved. User-specific memories and feedback remain separate from shared knowledge-bank sources and require their own privacy and consent controls.
